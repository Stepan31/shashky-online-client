About Shashky 3D

Shashky 3D is a free online 3D checkers game where you can enjoy the classic board game directly in your browser. No downloads or installations are required.

Play Checkers Your Way

Challenge the AI opponent and test your strategy, play with a friend on the same device, or join an online multiplayer game and compete against other players.

Classic Checkers in 3D

Enjoy a three-dimensional checkers board, interactive pieces, and a familiar checkers experience with different ways to play. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced player, Shashky 3D offers a simple way to enjoy checkers online.

Our Goal

Shashky 3D is an independent project created to make playing checkers online accessible, enjoyable, and easy for everyone. We continue working to improve the game and its multiplayer experience.
